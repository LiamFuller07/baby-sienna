## The process today

:::columns
1. **Intake.** The requester sends the request form by email, and Team A logs it and checks it is complete [T3 00:00:04; T3 00:00:19].
2. **Triage.** Finance reserves the budget as soon as the category is set [T3 00:01:05].
3. **Review.** Team C asks suppliers for quotes, usually three [T3 00:01:21], and Team B puts them into one approval pack [T3 00:01:52].
+++
4. **Approval.** Anything over 10,000 needs a finance sign-off after the approver signs [T3 00:02:10].
5. **Ordering.** Once the order is raised, Finance commits the budget [T3 00:02:41], and the records team keeps a copy [T3 00:05:57].
6. **Close.** Invoices are matched to the order and the delivery before payment [T3 00:03:30], and the request is closed [T3 00:03:45].
:::

**Where it slows.** Work goes back when a request is incomplete [T3 00:00:33] or the approver has comments [T3 00:02:26]. Quotes are compared by hand [T3 00:01:37], and chasing suppliers that have not confirmed takes most of a day each week [T3 00:03:14].
